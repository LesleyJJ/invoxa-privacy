Invoxa

Simple, fast invoicing for small businesses, right on your phone. No account, no sign-up, and it works fully offline.

What Invoxa does

Invoxa is a mobile app for Android and iOS that helps freelancers and small businesses create and send professional invoices. You add your business details, customers and items, choose a template, and Invoxa makes a clean PDF invoice you can share by email, messaging apps or print.

Professional invoices

Choose a template, add your logo and signature, and export a PDF in seconds.

Customers and items

Save your customers and items so you can reuse them on new invoices.

Fully offline

All your data is stored on your device. Invoxa has no servers and no accounts.

Automatic backups

Back up to a folder on your phone or to your own Google Drive, daily, weekly or monthly.

Pricing

Every feature is free and unlimited. The free version shows ads. An optional "Remove ads" subscription, bought through the App Store or Google Play, turns ads off.

How Invoxa uses your Google account

Signing in with Google is optional. It is used only if you choose Google Drive as a backup destination. Invoxa then saves backup files of your invoice data to an "Invoxa Backups" folder in your own Google Drive, so you can restore them on a new phone.

  • Invoxa requests only the limited drive.file permission. It can see and change only the files it creates, not the rest of your Drive.
  • Your Google email address is kept on your device only, to show which account is connected.
  • Your data is never sent to us, sold, or used for advertising.
  • You can disconnect at any time from the Backup screen in the app, or at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Invoxa's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Full details are in our Privacy Policy. Use of the app is covered by our Terms of Service.
