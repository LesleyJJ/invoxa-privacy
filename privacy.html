Invoxa Privacy Policy

Effective date: 25 November 2026

In short: Invoxa has no accounts and no servers. Your business profile, customers, invoices and templates stay on your device. We (the developer) do not collect, see or sell your personal data. The free version shows ads from Google AdMob, which collects some device data for advertising. Subscriptions go through the App Store or Google Play and RevenueCat. Google Drive backup is optional and saves only to your own Drive.

1. Who we are

Invoxa ("the app", "we", "us") is a mobile invoicing app published by John Lesley. If you have questions about this policy, contact us at support.invoxa.app@gmail.com.

2. Data stored on your device

Everything you enter in Invoxa is stored only in the app's private storage on your device. This includes your business profile, logo, signature, customers, invoices, items, templates and settings. We have no server, so this data is never sent to us. Invoice PDFs and backup files leave your device only when you choose to share, export or back them up.

Your device's operating system may include app data in its own device backups (for example iCloud or Android device backup). Those backups are handled by Apple or Google under their own terms.

3. Data handled by third parties

Each of these services processes data under its own privacy policy:

  • RevenueCat: revenuecat.com/privacy
  • Apple: apple.com/legal/privacy

Invoxa does not include any analytics or crash-reporting tools.

4. Advertising and your choices

  • EEA, UK and Switzerland: before any ads load, the app shows Google's consent form so you can choose whether ads may be personalised. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Ad privacy choices.
  • iOS: the app asks for permission under App Tracking Transparency before the Advertising ID can be used for tracking. You can change this in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Android: you can reset or delete your Advertising ID in your device's Settings → Privacy → Ads (the exact path depends on your device).
  • A subscription removes ads completely, and then no ads are requested.

5. Google Drive backup

If you choose Google Drive for backups, you sign in with Google only so the app can get permission to save files. Invoxa asks for the limited drive.file permission, which lets it see and change only the files it creates (the "Invoxa Backups" folder), not the rest of your Drive. The only account detail kept is your Google email address, stored on your device so the Backup screen can show which account is connected. Invoxa's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

You can disconnect at any time from the Backup screen. This revokes the app's access. Backup files already in your Drive stay there until you delete them. You can also remove access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

6. Data retention and deletion

Because your data is stored only on your device, you control it. You can delete all app data with Settings → Reset app data, or by uninstalling the app. Backup files you exported or saved to Google Drive stay wherever you put them until you delete them. Data held by AdMob, RevenueCat or the app stores is kept under their own policies. To request deletion of your RevenueCat purchase record, contact us and we will forward the request.

7. Security

All network communication by the services above uses encrypted connections (TLS). Subscription status cached on the device is kept in the operating system's secure storage. No method of storage or transfer is completely secure, so please protect your device with a passcode.

8. Children

Invoxa is a business tool and is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country). We do not knowingly collect data from children.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in the EU/UK or the CCPA in California), you may have rights to access, correct, delete or object to the processing of your personal data. Since we do not hold your personal data, most requests are handled directly in the app or with the third-party services listed above. We do not sell personal data. If you need help, contact us at support.invoxa.app@gmail.com.

10. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update this page and the effective date above. Significant changes will also be noted in the app's release notes.
